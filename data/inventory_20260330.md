# Example Inventory (Revision 30 March 2026)
[This inventory](./inventory_20260330.zip) contains 320,220 substances consolidated from the 47 lists below. It is provided for demonstration purposes only and is not guaranteed to be complete, accurate, or up to date. Users are strongly advised to verify all findings against the original source material whenever this inventory is used for analysis or screening.
